Vyral – AI-Powered Short-Form Video Generator SaaS Platform

By titansys

Vyral is a self-hosted AI short-form video generator you can sell as your own SaaS. Your customers type a prompt, paste a script, or drop in an article URL — and get back a finished vertical video with AI narration, word-timed captions, AI-generated scene images and a music bed. Subscription billing, a credit system, a full admin panel and a guided installer are already built, so you are running the business on day one instead of building it.

Perfect for entrepreneurs launching an AI video business, agencies adding short-form video to their services, and creators turning their content workflow into a product.

Why Choose Vyral?

  • Ready-to-Launch SaaS Business – Accept payments with PayPal and Bank Transfer from day one, with subscription plans and credit packs already built.
  • The Whole Video Engine – Script writing, AI voiceover, karaoke captions, AI scene images, music and final rendering. Nothing to bolt on.
  • You Set Every Price – Decide what each AI capability costs in credits. You pay the AI vendors wholesale and charge your customers retail.
  • Self-Hosted, No Platform Fees – Your server, your customers, your margins.
  • Sells Its Own Upgrades – Watermarks, resolution, frame rate, export quotas and AI video clips are all locked per plan, so the free tier does the selling for you.
  • Global From Day One – English and Japanese included, GDPR tools built in, and any language you add is supported (right-to-left included).

How You Make Money in 3 Steps

  1. Set your prices – Create subscription tiers, then set what each AI capability costs in credits: per script, per 1,000 characters of narration, per minute of transcription, per image quality tier and per second of AI video.
  2. Customers subscribe and spend credits – They pay through your gateways, then burn credits per video. Sell credit packs on top for customers who outgrow their plan.
  3. You keep the margin – Self-hosted means no platform cut. The credit system is how you recover your AI bills and price in your profit.

Three Ways In

  • Prompt to Video – One sentence becomes a full narrated short.
  • Script to Video – Your customer’s own words, kept exactly as written.
  • Article to Video – Paste a URL; the page is fetched and turned into a video.
  • Five Aspect Ratios – 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, 4:5 for feeds, plus 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9.
  • Auto Mode – Videos generated on a schedule from an RSS feed, a YouTube channel or a fixed prompt. Your customers wake up to new content.

Watch It Being Made

  • Live Progress – Customers watch each step happen as it happens. No page refreshing, no wondering whether it worked.
  • Nothing Is Lost – If a step fails, it picks up where it left off and the credits for it are returned automatically.

The Timeline Editor

  • Live Preview – A real video player in the browser shows exactly what will render, frame for frame.
  • Edit Any Scene – Rewrite the narration, regenerate the image, re-record the voice, reorder scenes, trim timing and add transitions.
  • Karaoke Captions – Captions are timed word by word, so they can highlight in sync, hide individual words and re-sync after an edit.
  • Music With Ducking – Drop in a track from your curated library, trim it on the timeline with a real waveform, and let it duck automatically under the narration.
  • Undo and Redo – Toolbar buttons and the keyboard shortcuts customers already know.
  • Reset to Original – One click puts a project back exactly as it was first generated, no matter how far it has been edited.
  • Only Pay for What Changed – Editing one scene regenerates only that scene and only charges for that. Nobody is billed for a full re-render because they fixed a typo.

You Control the AI — and Its Price

  • Mix Vendors Per Job – Scripts, narration, captions, images and video clips are each configured separately, so you can run narration on ElevenLabs while everything else stays on OpenAI. OpenAI, ElevenLabs and kie.ai are supported.
  • Set Every Price Yourself – Each capability’s cost in credits is yours to choose. Change a price and the next quote reflects it.
  • Quotes That Hold – The price a video is quoted at is locked in when the customer starts it, so changing your prices can never overcharge someone for work already under way.
  • Better AI for Better Plans – Point a premium plan at a stronger (or pricier) provider for any capability and you have a real quality ladder to sell.
  • Curated Voices – Pull in your ElevenLabs voice library, choose which voices customers may use, and let them listen before they generate.
  • No File Editing – All of it is configured in the admin panel. Your AI keys are stored scrambled and never reach anyone’s browser.

Monetization and Plan Gating

  • Credit System – Credits are held when a video starts and charged when it finishes. If anything fails, the credits go straight back. It stays correct no matter how many videos run at once.
  • Subscription Plans – Unlimited tiers, billed monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase, each with its own features and limits.
  • Gating That Sells Upgrades – Per plan: watermark on or off (with your own image and corner), maximum resolution, maximum frame rate and how many exports are allowed per period. Your free tier is a real free tier.
  • AI Video Clips as a Premium Tier – Animating a scene’s still image into a short AI video clip is its own per-plan feature, priced per second. It costs far more than a still image, which is exactly what makes it worth selling higher up.
  • Bring Your Own Key – A plan can let customers supply their own AI vendor key. Their videos then run on their own AI account and cost them only your flat per-video fee — a “use your own quota” tier you can charge for, with the vendor and model still your decision and their key stored scrambled.
  • Credit Packs – Sell one-off top-ups alongside recurring plans.
  • Payment Gateways – PayPal and Bank Transfer included. Stripe, Paystack, Razorpay, Crypto.com Pay and YooKassa available as paid plugin add-ons.
  • PDF Invoices – Generated automatically, and they follow your branding and your customer’s language.
  • Referral Program – Customers earn account credit for bringing in new sign-ups.
  • Transaction History – Every payment, subscription change and referral reward recorded, with detail pages for support and reconciliation.

Rendering You Can See

  • Render Builders – Register one or many render servers from the admin panel. Their health is actively checked, not toggled by hand, so the page tells you the truth.
  • Honest Queue States – A video waiting on a busy Builder is visibly waiting, not silently stuck.
  • It Fixes Itself – Finished videos are never lost, and anything that genuinely goes wrong is refunded automatically without you lifting a finger.
  • Scale Sideways – Add another Builder when rendering becomes the bottleneck.

Guided Onboarding

  • Five Tours Ship Ready – New customers are walked through the dashboard, the create page, the editor, their project library and billing.
  • You Write the Steps – Edit the tours, their wording and their order from the admin panel. No code, no deploy.
  • Replayable – Customers can restart a tour any time from the sidebar.

Content You Control

  • Art Styles – The visual looks customers pick from are editable templates. Add a style without touching code.
  • Caption Presets – Author the caption styles on offer — fonts, colours, highlighting and position.
  • Music Library – Upload and order the background tracks your customers choose from, so every video sounds like your brand.

Complete Admin Dashboard

  • User Management – Accounts, subscription status, credit balances and transaction history, plus impersonation so you can see exactly what a customer sees and switch back in one click.
  • Plans, Subscriptions and Transactions – Create tiers with limits, then track subscribers, renewals, cancellations and every payment.
  • Payment Plugins – Install, configure and switch gateways from the panel.
  • Landing Page Builder – Drag and drop, with separate content per language.
  • Languages – Add and manage interface languages.
  • Scheduled Tasks – Every background job listed, with one-click manual runs and logs.
  • System Settings – Email, sign-ups, real-time, referrals and GDPR configured at runtime, including full rebranding to your own name and logo.
  • Activity Log – A complete trail of admin actions.

Support Ticket System

  • Built-In Help Desk – Customers open tickets from their dashboard. No third-party tool needed.
  • Two-Way Messaging – A full conversation thread between customer and admin, with file attachments.
  • Admin Inbox – A dedicated view to read, assign, reply and close, with unread badges on both sides.
  • Notified Both Ways – Email and in-app notifications the moment a reply is posted.
  • Sellable – Support access is a per-plan feature, so you can reserve it for paying tiers.

Real-Time Notifications

  • Instant Updates – Finished videos, subscription changes and credit updates appear without a refresh.
  • Notification Centre – Customers review past notifications and mark them as read.
  • Self-Hosted – Live updates run on your own server out of the box. A hosted alternative is available as a setting if you would rather not run it yourself.

Multi-Language Support

  • English and Japanese Included – Both complete, so the language switcher works the moment you install.
  • Add Any Language – Manage languages from the admin panel; right-to-left languages are supported.
  • Customers Choose – Each customer picks their own language, and their emails and invoices follow it.

Privacy and Security

  • GDPR Ready – Cookie consent, data export, consent tracking and a grace period on account deletion.
  • Your Keys Stay Safe – Your AI and payment keys are stored scrambled and never sent to anyone’s browser.
  • Spam Protection – Google reCAPTCHA is built in to keep bots off your sign-up form.
  • Safe Uploads – Uploaded files are checked, cleaned and stored where only the right person can reach them.

Additional Features

  • Social Login – Google, Facebook and GitHub.
  • Search Engine Ready – Your pages look right when shared on social media, and Google is given everything it needs to find them. Private pages stay out of search results.
  • Branded Error Pages – Even the “page not found” screen carries your name and logo.
  • Make It Yours – Your name, logo and colours replace ours everywhere, from the admin panel to the emails.
  • Bundled Documentation – A full help site ships with the download, ready for you to hand to your customers.
  • Developer Friendly – If you hire someone to add features, the code ships with a complete worked example for them to follow.

Paid Add-Ons

The following payment gateways are sold separately as plugins:

  • Stripe
  • Paystack
  • Razorpay
  • Crypto.com Pay
  • YooKassa

System Requirements

Laravel Application:

  • PHP 8.4 or higher
  • MySQL 8.0+ or MariaDB 10.4+
  • Nginx or Apache
  • These PHP extensions, which nearly every host already has: BCMath, Ctype, cURL, DOM, Fileinfo, JSON, Mbstring, OpenSSL, PDO, Tokenizer, XML, and GD or Imagick

Render Builder (same server by default, or a separate one):

  • A Linux server. The setup script installs everything it needs, and a ready-to-run version is included, so there is nothing to build.
  • Node.js 20 or higher, which the Builder uses to put each video together.
  • Rendering is CPU and memory-hungry: 2 vCPU and 4 GB RAM is a sensible floor per video rendering at once.

Shared or VPS hosting works for the main application. A VPS is only needed for the Render Builder.
